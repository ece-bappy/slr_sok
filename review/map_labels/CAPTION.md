# Figure 3 caption (fig_map.pdf)

**Figure 3.** The 291 included studies by publication year. (a) Threat each study targets; "other threats" groups web attacks (10), malicious servers (6), anomaly detection with no named threat (7) and other (8). (b) Model class of the study's own method, as a share of that year's studies; "not stated" means the abstract does not name the model. Labels come from titles and abstracts: two LLMs labelled every study blind with the same rulebook, and a human decided all 119 disputed labels and checked 90 agreed labels (1 wrong). *2026 runs to the search date (26 Oct 2026).

Source: `review/map_labels/final_labels.csv`; script `scripts/fig_map.py`; agreement and human check in `review/map_labels/agreement.md`.
